Financial
Income gaps, salary resets, the ROI of reskilling.
Personal
Family, partner, and the life balance you already built.
Health
Physical burnout, mental load, identity crisis.
Career
Seniority reset, credibility rebuilt from zero.

Part Two

What will it feel like?

Three challenges nobody warns you about.
And how to survive each one.
What will it feel like? - Challenge 1

Skills gap & constant reskilling pressure

You have more experience than you think.
You just don't know how to sell it yet.
This is a language problem, not an experience problem.
01You can't reframe it.
It rarely maps 1:1, but it transfers. Learn the translation.
02You undersell it.
Most pivoters undersell exactly where they should oversell.
03You let AI hand you a roadmap.
A roadmap is not a skill. Use AI to close the gap, not to describe it.

What will it feel like? - Challenge 2

Job market brutality & hiring dysfunction

The market is broken.
Focus on the controllables.
You cannot fix hiring. You can fix what you take away from every no.
01You don't audit your failures.
Find the leak: resume, interview prep, confidence. Do an RCA and course correct.
02You can't manage your rejections.
Acknowledge the failure.
Use grit and resilience to stay motivated.
03You bet your pivot on a trend.
Some roles might die sooner. Map how the role will evolve with time and changing technologies.
What will it feel like? - Challenge 2

Analyze the rejection. Fix the right thing.

Story
If they did not understand your journey...
Rewrite your story.
Skill
If they did not acknowledge your expertise...
Go build the evidence.
Signal
If they did not see you...
Get visible.
What will it feel like? - Challenge 3

“Being delulu is not the solulu in the AI world”

Stop consuming AI without creating. Every build is a win.
Build failed
You learned something.
Build good
You solved a problem for yourself.
Build great
You solved a problem for everyone.
Try → Iterate → Repeat

Part Three

How to build credibility for your pivot role?

The BRICK framework.
How to build credibility for your pivot role? - The framework

Earn the role you’ve never held, brick by brick.

B
Base Knowledge
Leverage what you already know. Your past experience is not “irrelevant”; it’s transferable.
R
Run Simulation
Create small, intentional projects. Demonstration beats theoretical knowledge.
I
Implement Execution
Seek messy, high-leverage opportunities where decisions matter. Credibility grows where outcomes are real, not hypothetical.
C
Claim Ownership
Highlight moments where you took responsibility over decisions, not titles.
K
Key Impact
Translate your work into measurable results. Impact is the proof that your stack works and makes your credibility undeniable.
